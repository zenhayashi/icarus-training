SEC 0303

H.E.L.I.O.S.

Our assistant, made for Icarus by Fenfast. Free for every member. Ask it in plain English. Agreed

What it does for us Added

  • Trade: best hauls for your ship, round trips that never fly empty, where to sell
  • Mining and salvage: where to mine, which laser and modules, salvage gear
  • Ships and gear: stats, loadouts, where to buy, ship against ship
  • Our org: who has which ship, work orders, reminders, group finder, polls
  • Patches: what changed in the latest update
Talking points
  • Prices come from UEX, reported by players, so they can be a little old.
  • Everything else is read from the game's own files.
  • It never messages you first: a person from the org says hello.

How to use it

  • Go to #helios-chat and start with "Helios, ..."
  • Type / to see its commands, or /commands for every one with an example
  • Stuck? #how-to-use-helios explains it
  • Something wrong or missing? #helios-feedback, or tag Taz or Fenfast

Try these Added

"Helios, what is the best haul for 96 SCU?"
"Helios, where do I buy the RSI Aurora Mk II?"
"Helios, where is the best place to mine Aluminum?"
Talking points
  • We want you to break it. Every problem found gets fixed.
  • Ask like you would ask a person: no special wording needed.
SEC 0404

The H.E.L.I.O.S. app

A companion app for Windows that puts H.E.L.I.O.S. on your screen while you play. Agreed: it comes later, at a higher rank

Month one: InitiateThe app is not open yet. H.E.L.I.O.S. in the server answers the same questions.
A month in: MemberThe app opens. Say "Helios, I need a setup code" in #helios-chat.
ThenThe admins approve it and H.E.L.I.O.S. sends you the code in a direct message.
InstallEnter the code, choose a password. The app updates itself.
Added
Talking points
  • It opens at the Member rank, about a month after joining.
  • Nothing to do until then: ask in the server.
  • Windows only.

What the app gives you

  • A small orb that opens a heads-up display over the game: trade routes, mining, patch news, the org inventory, a marketplace, and chat
  • Talk to it: Alt Shift H, ask out loud, and it answers out loud
  • Look at my screen: Alt Shift V sends one picture of your game with your question
  • Log items for the org: Alt Shift F8 starts the hover log, then point at items

Everything runs only when you press something. Screenshots are not kept, and your wallet only ever shows in an org total, never under your name. Added

Talking points
  • The hotkeys are meant to work from inside the game.
  • Everything in the app is optional.
  • Ships come from Fleetyards, so nothing is entered twice.

SEC 0606

Work orders

For now we run them with H.E.L.I.O.S.: it is simpler while we grow and learn. Once it works with Fleetyards we will pick one standard. Agreed: Taz and Fenfast

Ask/request something from a team
ApproveAn admin approves it and sets a time
AcceptPress Accept on the card in #work-orders
CompletePress Complete when it is done

Want a job to take? /workorder list shows what is open. Added

Talking points
  • A work order is a job for the crew, shown as a card.
  • Anyone can request. Admins approve.

Dispatch

  • An admin sends a job straight to the crew with /dispatch
  • They say what it is, which team, who it is for if anyone, when it is due, and how urgent: routine, priority or urgent
  • Dispatches and completions are logged in #work-dispatch so everyone can see what is going on
Added
Talking points
  • Request is you asking. Dispatch is the org asking you.
SEC 0707

Where we are heading

Today the work runs through work orders and dispatch. As we grow it becomes guilds with classes and instructors. This is the Guild Training Plan, summarized: a proposal the admins are still deciding on, and nothing opens until we have grown enough.

Medicalfirst, for everyone
Miners
Salvagers
Haulers
Combat
Crafters
Engineers
Refuelers
  • You pick one main guild and up to two sub guilds
  • Classes are short and hands-on: 45 to 60 minutes, nobody only watches
  • The path: Orientation, Basic class, Advanced class, Instructor, Leader. Anyone can stop at any step
Talking points
  • Eight guilds are planned. Medical goes first and everyone takes its basic class.
  • Every guild gets two trained instructors so no class depends on one person.
  • The full plan is on the Guild Training Plan page: zenhayashi.github.io/icarus-training.
  • This is a proposal: do not promise dates.

Your first steps

Your welcome message in the server ticks each one off as you go.
