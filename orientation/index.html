SEC 0303

H.E.L.I.O.S.

Our assistant. Made for Icarus by Fenfast, and free for every member. Ask it things in plain English. Agreed: how to use it and what it can do for us

What it can do for us Added

  • Trade: best hauls for your ship, round trips that never fly empty, where to sell, price history.
  • Mining and salvage: where to mine an ore, which laser, modules and gadgets to use, salvage gear.
  • Ships and gear: stats, loadouts, where to buy a ship or an item, ship against ship, weapons, armor, missiles.
  • Our org: who has which ship, work orders, reminders, group finder, polls, what is coming up.
  • Patches: what changed in the latest update.

How to use it Agreed

  • Talk to it in #helios-chat. Start with "Helios, ..." and ask like you would ask a person.
  • Type / to see its commands. /commands lists every one with an example, and #how-to-use-helios explains them.
  • There is a companion app you can download later, once you reach a higher rank.
  • Something wrong or missing? Post it in #helios-feedback, or tag Taz or Fenfast. We want you to break it: every problem found gets fixed.

Try these Added

  • "Helios, what is the best haul for 96 SCU?"
  • "Helios, where do I buy the RSI Aurora Mk II?"
  • "Helios, where is the best place to mine Aluminum?"
  • "Helios, what are the stats for the P4-AR?"

Prices come from UEX, reported by players, so they can be a little old. Everything else comes from the game's own files. H.E.L.I.O.S. never messages you first: a person from the org says hello.

SEC 0404

Fleetyards

We use it for scheduling events and recording our fleet stats, for now, until org play arrives and we can share ships and log out in each other's ships. Agreed: Taz

What to do Added

  • Join our fleet: fltyrd.net/fi/2b800940
  • Add your ships to My Hangar and sync your RSI hangar.
  • Press Verify with Fleetyards on your welcome message (H.E.L.I.O.S. also ticks it when your name matches).
  • You only enter your ships once: H.E.L.I.O.S. reads our fleet from Fleetyards, which is how it can tell you who has a Perseus.

What else it can do for us Agreed: add what Taz has not seen yet

  • Events and calendar: create events with slots and teams, sign-ups, reminders, a calendar feed.
  • Fleet roster and hangars: who is in the fleet, which ships we own, fleet statistics, member health checks.
  • Logistics: the items the org holds, in inventories with stock.
  • Contracts: jobs with items, a crew and payouts, which is what our work orders map to.
  • Squadrons, roles, blueprints and invites for when we grow.

H.E.L.I.O.S. can read all of this today. Writing to Fleetyards (creating events and contracts for us) starts once Fleetyards approves our app.

SEC 0505

Work orders and dispatch

For now we run them with H.E.L.I.O.S.: it is simpler while we grow and learn. Fleetyards has a nice interface, and once H.E.L.I.O.S. works with it we will pick one standard. Agreed: Taz and Fenfast

Work orders Added

  • A work order is a job for the crew, shown as a card in #work-orders with Accept and Complete buttons.
  • Need something done or supplied? Use /request to ask a team for it. An admin approves it and sets a time, and it becomes a work order.
  • Want a job to take? /workorder list shows what is open. Press Accept on the card, and Complete when it is done.

Dispatch Added

  • Dispatch is how an admin sends a job straight to the crew with /dispatch: what it is, which team, who it is for if anyone, when it is due, and how urgent (routine, priority or urgent).
  • Dispatches and their completions are logged in #work-dispatch, so everyone can see what is going on.
SEC 0606

Where we're heading

Today the work runs through work orders and dispatch. As Icarus grows, it becomes guilds with classes and instructors. This is the Guild Training Plan, summarized. It is a proposal the admins are still deciding on, and nothing opens until we have grown enough.

The short version

  • Eight guilds: Medical, Miners, Salvagers, Haulers, Combat, Crafters, Engineers and Refuelers. Medical goes first, and everyone takes its basic class.
  • You pick one main guild and up to two sub guilds.
  • Classes are short and hands-on: 45 to 60 minutes, small groups, nobody only watches.
  • Every guild has two trained instructors, so no class depends on one person.

SEC 0707

Your first steps

Everything above in order. Your welcome message in the server ticks each one off as you go. Added

  1. Accept the rules. Press I agree to the rules under the rules post.
  2. Introduce yourself. Press the Introduce yourself button and fill in the short form: what you play, the ships you fly, what you would like to try.
  3. Apply to our RSI org (ICLS). Then press I've applied. An admin confirms it.
  4. Join our Fleetyards fleet and add your ships.
  5. Say hello to H.E.L.I.O.S. in #helios-chat.
  6. Hop into voice chat any time. Someone will gladly party up.
